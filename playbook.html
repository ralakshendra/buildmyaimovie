THE WORKFLOW

Research Before You Build.

Follow the same order used in the session so your channel decisions are grounded in evidence before you start designing the brand.

01

Niche + Proof

Study viral channels and use sold-channel examples as proof-of-concept signals when validating a category.

02

God Prompt

Edit the research variables in Google Docs, then use Kimi to investigate the market, channels and opportunities.

04

Profile Picture

Use Section B inputs B1, B2 and B3 in ChatGPT image generation with your channel name.

05

Channel Banner

Use Canva to turn the channel identity and color direction into the first YouTube channel banner.
