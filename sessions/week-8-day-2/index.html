FROM FOOTAGE TO REVIEW

Follow one effect through a complete test.

The source shows several transformations. Pick the one your clip and references can support; you do not need to reproduce every example in one assignment.

STEP 1

Choose one transformation and a base shot

Select one effect the available footage can support: object replacement, character and scene transformation, full scene redesign, motion transfer, character replacement, or reference-to-video. Identify the original clip and state what must change and what should remain stable before opening a generation tool.

Review: The chosen effect is specific, the base shot is identified, and the intended change is separated from details that should be preserved.

STEP 2

Prepare the reference set

Capture a useful frame when the source describes a screenshot workflow. Prepare only the references required by your route: subject, prop, costume, background, plain matte or other named elements. If using the reference-to-video route, the source demonstrates removing a subject from a frame, using the clip to ask Gemini for a prompt with explicit reference anchors, and attaching the prepared elements in Vidu Q2.

Review: Every reference has a clear role and agrees with the requested subject, scene, framing and motion.

STEP 3

Choose the video method for the transformation

Match the method to the job. The source pairs object replacement with Kling 1 Video-to-Video, a boxing scene with OpenArt video-to-video and Kling 3.0, scene replacement with Seedance 2.0, motion transfer with OpenArt Motion Sync and DreamActor M2.0 or Kling 3.0, character replacement with OpenArt Replace Body or Kling 01, and element-based reference-to-video with Vidu Q2. Treat the reported winners and issues as class observations rather than guarantees.

Review: The selected method supports the intended transformation and uses the references that method expects.

STEP 4

Generate one test and inspect the full clip

Use the closest complete source prompt or adapt a working copy for your own footage. Keep the transformation request focused and name the details the source says to preserve, such as face, body motion, timing, camera position, lighting, background or props. Review the clip from beginning to end instead of judging only its strongest frame.

Review: The test applies the requested change while retaining the selected identity, movement, timing and framing constraints.

STEP 5

Record the result and the first correction

Compare the generated clip with the source. Note changes that worked, any identity or scene drift, lighting or relighting problems, unstable props, unwanted background changes, and motion that no longer matches. Save the selected clip and its source/reference links in the Homework Doc, then state the first correction supported by what you observed.

Review: A result link, selected method, source/reference links, review notes and evidence-based first correction are recorded.

MATCH THE METHOD TO THE CHANGE

Seven routes from the source packet

1. Replace one object

Make a candle reference from the pen shot with Nano Banana Pro, then apply it with Kling 1 Video-to-Video. The supplied video prompt asks to preserve the rest of the original clip.

2. Add a character context

Use a frame from the source clip, create a boxing-scene reference with Nano Banana Pro, then animate it in OpenArt video-to-video with Kling 3.0.

3. Replace a scene and outfit

Prepare the changed background, costume and prop reference, then use the source frame in Seedance 2.0 video-to-video. The prompt calls out face and camera position as details to keep.

4. Redesign the scene

The car-interior example changes the setting and lighting while preserving the character’s face and body movement.

5. Transfer motion

OpenArt Motion Sync compares DreamActor M2.0 with Kling 3.0. The source reports Kling won in that class comparison.

6. Replace the character

OpenArt Replace Body and Kling 01 are compared. The source reports Kling 01 won and notes relighting issues.

7. Build with separate elements

Prepare a background, character and screenshot reference. The source demonstrates removing the original character with Adobe Firefly, asking Gemini for a Vidu Q2 prompt with reference anchors, then generating with the prepared elements.

Class observation: Tool names, reported winners and issues describe the supplied demonstration. They are not guarantees about current availability or results on different footage.

Sample Vidu Q2 elements-to-video prompt
Generate a cinematic action sequence combining the environment of [background_image_ref] with the exact subject from [character_image_ref], strictly adhering to the motion, timing, and framing of [screen_grab_ref].
Setting: A dramatic night scene filled with thick, atmospheric smoke and dust. The background features an old, gothic stone church structure outlined by draped strings of glowing yellow fairy lights. The dusty ground is scattered with distinct, intense pools of burning fire. The ambient lighting is warm, driven by flickering orange and yellow firelight against the dark night.
Character: The subject is an Indian man strictly matching [character_image_ref]—it is crucial that his facial features, specifically his clear aviator glasses, mustache, and goatee, remain consistent without morphing. He wears a highly detailed, tactical full-body red armor suit with raised muscle panels, glowing red-orange light strips, and a metallic gold "E" emblem on the chest.
Action & Camera: The scene begins dynamically as the character drops rapidly down from out of frame. He lands heavily in the center of the frame in a deep, bent-knee crouch, kicking up a cloud of dust on impact. From this crouch, he immediately rises and squares his shoulders directly to the camera, entering a tense, wide-legged power stance with arms slightly flared out. Once fully standing and front-facing, he aggressively uses his hands to pull a red cloth mask up to cover his mouth and nose, locking his eyes into an intense, piercing stare directly at the lens. The camera executes a smooth, continuous dolly-in (pushing forward) throughout the entire sequence, closing in on his face and upper body as the ground fires continuously burn around him.
