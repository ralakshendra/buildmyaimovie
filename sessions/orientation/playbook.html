Put the lesson into practice

STEP 1

Set up your learning routine

Pin your current cohort group so updates are easy to find. Save the shared resources. Contact your assigned facilitator first when you have a doubt. Bring a video you can replay.

Review: You can find the current group, your resources and a video to study.

STEP 2

Learn the visual vocabulary

Use the God Movie Maker's Playbook to distinguish framing from camera angle, movement from a still frame, lighting from colour grading, and lens characteristics from acting. Start with camera and framing, movement, lighting, optics, colour and performance.

Review: For each term you choose, point to visible evidence in the frame or clip.

STEP 3

Decode one scene with your group

Choose a group leader to share a video. Pause on a reference frame and discuss camera angle, framing, point of view, movement, lighting, lens characteristics, colour and acting. Replay the clip when a still frame cannot answer the question.

Review: Your group can explain its observations and identify what remains uncertain.

STEP 4

Capture and analyse at least ten scenes

Choose a video. Capture a reference frame for each of at least ten scenes. In your own homework copy, record its source and timestamp, describe the visuals, and identify camera, movement, lighting, lens characteristics, colour and acting. Use the script template's VISUALS and JSON PROMPTS columns as a structural reference.

Review: At least ten records each contain a reference frame, a traceable source and a complete written breakdown.

STEP 5

Verify the uncertain details

Look up unfamiliar shot terms with Google or YouTube. If you still need help, use ChatGPT or Gemini with the reference PDF and your frame or clip. Compare the suggestion with what you can actually observe, then revise your breakdown.

Review: Your final notes separate observed details from estimates; no exact lens or movement is claimed without evidence.

When you get stuck

I cannot name the angle.

Compare the frame with the vocabulary reference. Search Google or YouTube for examples, then use the closest term you can justify.

I cannot tell whether this is a zoom or a dolly.

Replay the clip. A screenshot only captures one moment. If the movement is still unclear, mark it uncertain rather than making a confident guess.

The assistant gives a different answer.

Compare its answer with your frame, clip and reference definitions. Use ChatGPT or Gemini to investigate a doubt; retain the evidence and your own review.
