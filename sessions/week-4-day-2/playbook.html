05
Filmora or an editor with equivalent controls

Find your best ten seconds

Bring in

Approved clips and the five-beat edit plan.

Prompt or recipe

No generation prompt. The edit recipe is: clear shot jobs, usable frames, deliberate speed contrast, clean cuts.

Exact steps

  1. Set a vertical project, save it and import the approved clips.
  2. Place macro, form, technology, combining and reveal beats on the timeline. Use the plan as a guide, not a reason to keep weak frames.
  3. Trim bad entrances and exits. Use clean hard cuts rather than adding transitions to hide problems.
  4. Open Speed → Speed Ramping and tune the curve to each move. Raise overly slow sections toward 1x as demonstrated.
  5. Reverse the side-exit clip if using that workaround. Keep the optional outro outside the ten-second core.

Output

A saved project with a ten-second core picture edit.

How you know

The phone remains recognizable and every shot adds momentum.

If it fails

Trim the weak section, adjust the curve and replace a clip that cannot preserve the product.

Prompt or recipe

No generation prompt. Use the sound map and verified source searches below.

Exact steps

  1. Audition tech-house, cyberpunk or minimal electronic tracks. Pick a useful 10 to 15-second section and mark its beats.
  2. Align macro movement, lens assembly and the reveal to those beat points. Trim the final audio to the ad.
  3. Place quiet texture on the macro, clicks on lens movement, a controlled transition whoosh and one final hit.
  4. Lower the music under key SFX, keep reverb restrained and finish with a clean tail.
  5. Confirm the platform’s current licence if you will publish the film.

Output

Ten seconds of sound where the clicks come through and the music fits.

How you know

Clicks land on visible actions and the reveal has one clear payoff.

If it fails

Remove competing effects, shorten their tails and rebalance the music.

EDITING HELP

Find the useful seconds.

Trimming, colour and export checks
  • Trim precisely: zoom into the timeline. Mark usable in and out points where your editor supports them; I and O were demonstrated in class.
  • Close the gap: after deleting weak frames, check that neighbouring clips still meet and the sound stays in sync. Ripple editing closes a gap; verify the result.
  • Match first: correct exposure and colour differences between clips before adding a creative look. Keep the orange finish consistent.
  • Export for the project: preserve the vertical timeline and choose settings appropriate to your source footage. The general handbook’s platform presets are reference examples, not compulsory class settings.
  • Keep the project: save before major edits, use clear clip names, and keep the finished project with its media and export.
