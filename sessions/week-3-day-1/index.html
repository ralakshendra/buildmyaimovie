03 · Choose Your Method

Fast vs structured.

Fast option

  • Generate a screenplay directly from the brand brief.
  • Useful when speed matters or the LLM already knows the full brand story through an existing workflow.
  • Risk: the model may not know the backstory, emotion or connection point behind the story.

Structured option used today

  • Build the character sheet and mood board first.
  • Give the LLM visual context for the mood and tone of the ad.
  • Use that context to generate and review the screenplay.
  • More setup, but stronger creative alignment.
