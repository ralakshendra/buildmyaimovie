Session overview

Alexx Roy walks you through the whole AI Moviemaking production loop in one sitting: show, then you do, then you work alone.

The strategy behind everything

  • 80/20 rule: focus on the 20% of work that gives 80% of the result.
  • That 20% is: copy a viral video format closely, using the research and prompts from the previous session.
  • Volume plan: post 100 videos that replicate viral formats. About 80 will fail and about 20 will win.
  • Replicate the winners: find the 20 that work, check their topic and script, then change the hooks and stories and make the next 100 like them.
  • These are low-effort videos. Small mistakes are fine. Speed and volume are the point.

Today's agenda, in order

  1. Visual mastery with Google Flow (Veo 3.1) and its settings
  2. Types of videos you can animate
  3. Script generation quick hack
  4. Image generation hack
  5. Voiceovers with ElevenLabs

Step 1: Visual mastery in Google Flow (Veo 3.1)

Flow is where you turn images and JSON prompts into video clips.

Set up a project
  1. Open Flow and log in. Your existing videos live in Projects.
  2. Click New project. This workspace is where the work happens.
  3. Choose Video, then pick a mode:
    • Frames: give one image and it creates a video from it. Use this now.
    • Ingredients: give several items (for example a person, a computer, a mouse) and it combines them into one video. Saved for a future session.
  4. Choose 9:16 for YouTube Shorts (16:9 is the horizontal option).
  5. Choose how many outputs per prompt (1x to 4x). Flow is a machine and can fail, so 2x is recommended. With few credits, use 1x.
  6. Choose the model (table below).

Make your first video from an image and JSON prompts
  1. In Flow click Start, then Upload image. Upload your first-scene image.
  2. Paste one to three JSON prompts (the ones from the previous session).
  3. Press Enter. Flow generates the clips.
  4. Extend the video: click the generated clip, copy the next scene prompts (for example scenes 3 and 4), paste them into the extend box and press Enter. Repeat until all scenes are done.
  5. Download: Download, then Full video at 720p to get one combined file, or download the individual clips separately for editing.
If later scenes look wrong: take a screenshot of the frame you want, then make that scene as image to video instead of prompt to video. You get more control.
Perfect is not the goal. Curves and details will be imperfect. Generate, then trim in the editor. Start with the easiest thing and raise the difficulty slowly.
Fixes for common Flow problems
  • Credits ran out: use the Jio subscription offer (a roughly ₹349 to ₹360 monthly recharge gives about 1,000 AI credits). Do not buy a SIM just for this. Otherwise contact the admin team for an account, or use another account (each Pro account has 1,000 credits).
  • Two videos every time: that is the outputs-per-prompt setting. Change it.
  • Repeated videos in a project: normal. Just ignore or delete extras.
  • Error on generate, or a person in the image is rejected: read the error, check whether your inputs and intended use are supported, and use the provider’s help if the restriction remains. Do not keep spending credits or change identity wording to work around a rejection.
  • Can't find saved videos: they are inside your Flow project.
  • Google Vids vs Flow: stop using Google Vids. It is a beginner step. Use Flow.

Step 2: Types of videos you can make

DIRECTOR’S NOTE

Give the scene a voice.

Choose dialogue or narration to support the story. Use the examples below to decide.

Know the three styles. Stick to the first two.

  • Characters talk to each other on screen (for example a talking monkey reporter in a 3D pixel style).
  • How: use the same JSON prompt process, and ask the AI to give the script with dialogue. Prompt: "Make this dialogue based". Copy the dialogue into the JSON prompt, then generate.
  • If dialogue does not fit in 8 seconds, break it into parts.
  • For now, use Veo 3.1 itself to generate dialogue. A separate masterclass covers five ways to do dialogue.
  • To turn dialogue off: tell the JSON prompt "do not add dialogue".
Two people in one scene, wrong person speaks: label each person clearly (for example "Person A, black shirt" and "Person B, gray shirt") and put each person's lines under their label. It may take one or two generations to work. Another method: use a wide angle shot and a close-up for each character, and cut to the close-up when that person speaks.
  • The video plays with music while a narrator tells the story. This is the format of the Mongolia exercise.
  • How: ask the AI "Give me the entire narrator script in Hindi" (or your language).
  • Easy to produce at scale.
  • Only music, visuals and on-screen text.
  • Not recommended. It is hard to make well. Stick to dialogue or voiceover.

Start with the story beat.

Step 3: Script and image hacks

Script workflow
  1. Take the link of a viral video.
  2. Put it into Gemini and have it turn the video into a transcript.
  3. Analyse the transcript’s structure, then write an original script with your own story, wording and contribution. Use owned or permitted visual references.
Image-to-video hack
  1. Choose a first-frame reference you own or have permission to use.
  2. Upload it into Flow.
  3. Add the JSON prompts and generate the video.
Replicate the winners. After posting many videos, see which 20% perform, check topic and script, then make the next batch like them.

Step 4: Voiceovers with ElevenLabs

DIRECTOR’S NOTE

Listen before you export.

Check the pacing, pronunciation and emotion before downloading your voiceover.

Prepare your voiceover, then check its publishing license.

Publishing note · checked 10 October 2026: ElevenLabs free-plan audio is for noncommercial use and requires attribution. For commercial publishing, confirm the license for the plan and feature used to generate the audio; Beta Services have separate restrictions. Read the provider’s publishing guidance before uploading.

  1. Search for ElevenLabs and sign up with your Google account.
  2. Open Text to speech.
  3. Paste your script. Keep it close to one minute (about 1,500 characters for the demo).
  4. Click Voices. Search "narrator" and set Language (Hindi, Bengali and many regional languages exist). Listen and pick a calm, serious narrative voice.
  5. Choose the V2 model if you like it, and adjust speed slightly. Do not push values too far.
  6. Click Generate speech, listen, then Download.
Good to know: Use your own voice later, with a different model. If your account says "unusual activity detected", pause and follow the provider’s official help or contact support. Do not switch accounts to work around a restriction. To redo a narration, regenerate in ElevenLabs.

Step 5: Basic editing in Filmora

DIRECTOR’S NOTE

The story comes together in the edit.

Trim the clips and line up your visuals with the voiceover, one scene at a time.

Only the essentials. A 15 to 20 minute advanced Filmora tutorial is included in the homework.

Create the project and import media
  1. Open Wondershare Filmora and choose 9:16 for vertical Shorts (16:9 for horizontal). Click Short video project.
  2. Know the three areas: the media panel (assets), the timeline (cuts and edits) and the preview.
  3. Click Import and bring in your video clips and your voiceover audio.
  4. If a prompt says resolution or frame rate doesn't match, choose Match with media.
  5. Drag the clips and audio onto the timeline.
Shape the story on the timeline
  1. The voiceover and the clips will not line up at first. This is normal.
  2. Use Split (the cut or razor tool) to cut a clip where you need to.
  3. Move each clip to match what the narrator says.
  4. Aim for a good storyline, not perfection. Around 70% of the film is done once clips match the voiceover.
  5. For missing shots, decide what should be there, generate those clips in Flow, and add them.
Magnet trick: clips snap to each other when placed below. Place a clip on the layer above it to move freely.
Quick fixes
  • Mute the clip's own audio: click the clip, open Audio, set the volume to 0 (or drag the line on the clip's audio wave).
  • Remove the Veo watermark: click the clip, open Video, increase the size so the mark goes off screen. You can place your own logo on top.
  • Short too short: try AI extend.
  • Download: you can edit a full video or the frame-by-frame clips. Try both.
  • Do not use Canva for this stage. Filmora is the tool.
  • Filmora login or watermark problems: contact the admin team.
Length: Alexx suggests under 59 seconds. Shorts can go up to 3 minutes, but shorter stories are slightly better.

Step 6: Create and launch your YouTube channel

The first thing to do today. Follow in order.

A. Create the account safely
  1. Create a brand new Google account (personal use, not a business account).
  2. Save your email, password and set up two-step recovery. Creators with 10,000 subscribers have lost channels by forgetting these.
  3. Sign in on YouTube, open the profile icon and choose YouTube Studio.
  4. Upload your channel picture, set the name and a handle (add an underscore or number if taken, the handle is not critical), then click Create channel.
Account safety: keep each channel on its own account, in separate browsers, and on different IP addresses if possible. One phishing link can take down several linked accounts, and a strike on one channel may hurt others. Check links carefully, even ones that look official.
  • An old Google account works only if it has no channel yet. The channel name and handle can be changed later.
B. Customize the channel
  1. Click Customize channel, then Continue.
  2. Banner: upload it. Make future banners 2560 by 1440 so they fit.
  3. Description: open your Kimi channel plan, go to the SEO and AEO section, copy the channel description, remove the keywords part and the repeated welcome lines.
    • SEO is search engine optimization. AEO is AI engine optimization, so AI tools can also recommend your channel.
  4. Put the SEO and AEO block on top of the description.
  5. Skip external links for now. Add them once your channel has grown, or YouTube may think you divert traffic.
  6. Add an email address for business inquiries.
  7. Video watermark is optional. Add your channel logo (resize if too large) later.
  8. Click Publish, then Go to channel.
C. Settings for discovery and workflow
  • Settings → Channel: set your country of residence (Google can tell, so be careful when targeting a different audience).
  • Paste your master keywords from the Mother Blueprint (tier 1, 2 and 3). Short-tail and long-tail keywords mix to surface your channel. Delete some long-tail ones, they matter least.
  • Upload defaults: set once (for example "made for kids") so you do not repeat it each time.
  • Permissions: invite editors or managers with an email and a role.
  • Agreements: nothing to do for now.
D. Upload your first Short
  1. Click Create, then Select files, and choose your video.
  2. Add a short title. Description and tags matter little for Shorts.
  3. Audience: choose No, it's not made for kids unless your channel is truly for kids.
  4. Altered content: mark Yes if it shows a realistic altered person or scene.
  5. Keep the standard YouTube license and comments on. Category (Entertainment, Education) does not matter much.
  6. Skip related video for now, pass the checks, choose Public and Publish.
Early traction: Alexx shares channel links in the group and members subscribe, like and comment for each other. This is not a long-term strategy, just a way to get started.

Rules and answers from the Q&A

  • Length: keep Shorts under 59 seconds. Shorter is better.
  • Where to post: on the YouTube channels you created.
  • Thumbnails: Shorts do not need them. Thumbnails come later in the cohort.
  • Don't share your videos on your own WhatsApp or Instagram: YouTube may see you already have traffic and give you less. This is Alexx's experience from testing, not YouTube's official policy. Let videos grow organically.
  • Copyright: permission and originality matter even for one upload. Minor changes do not guarantee permission or monetization. Read YouTube’s original-content requirements.
  • Currency: INR is fine until you monetize.
  • Channel for kids: choose "made for kids" and set it once in upload defaults.
  • Automation tools: not now. Go through the manual process first so you build the habit. A few AI agents will be covered later.
  • Use Flow, ElevenLabs and Filmora by need: Flow can output dialogue and music, so the others are use-case based.
  • Own characters: a later module. For now study successful formats and create your own version using original scripts and permitted assets.
  • Support: tool and account problems go to the admin team, who will help within office hours. Expect slight delays while the team grows.
