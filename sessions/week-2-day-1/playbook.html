Editing Essentials: finish one short first

Course guidance: arrange your strongest shots, trim unusable sections, align the narration, balance voice and music, then export and watch the complete file. Save both the editable project and the checked export. Fix black gaps, missing media, clipped speech or an unfinished ending before starting the publishing loop. Repeat this basic workflow in later projects; Week 7 develops advanced editing.
